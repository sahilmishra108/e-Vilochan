# User Manual

## Getting Started

### Roles
*   **Hospital Admin**: Manage ICUs, Staff, and view all patients.
*   **Doctor**: View assigned patients, prescribe meds, view vitals, chat.
*   **Staff**: Monitor vitals, update patient status, chat.

### Logging In
1.  Go to the login page.
2.  Enter your registered email and password.
3.  Click "Login".

## Dashboard Navigation

### 1. Dashboard (Home)
This is your command center.
*   **Vitals Alerts**: See real-time alerts for critical patients at the top.
*   **Patient Overview**: A card view of all patients in your ICU/Hospital.
*   **Statistics**: Charts showing admission trends and vital sign averages.

### 2. Patient Records
*   **List View**: See a tabular list of all patients.
*   **Admit Patient**: (Admin/Staff only) Click "Add Patient" to register a new admission.
*   **Tabs**: Switch between "Active Patients" and "Discharged".

### 3. Patient Monitor (The "Camera" Tab)
This is the core feature for gathering vitals.
1.  Select a patient from the dropdown.
2.  **Live Camera**: Allow browser camera access. The AI will scan the patient's face/monitor.
3.  **Start Capture**: Click to begin auto-extraction (runs every 30s).
4.  **Video Upload**: Toggle to "Video Mode" to upload a pre-recorded video file for analysis.

## Clinical Workflow

### Viewing Patient Details
Click on any patient card or name to open their **Patient Details** page.
*   **Live Vitals**: Real-time graph of HR and SpO2.
*   **History**: A table of all past vital readings.
*   **Prescriptions**: View or Add (Doctor only) medications.
*   **AI Summary**: Click "Generate SBAR" to get an AI-generated clinical summary.

### Chat & Collaboration
1.  Click the "Chat" icon (or "Consult" button).
2.  **Hospital Chat**: General channel for the whole hospital.
3.  **ICU Chat**: Focused channel for your specific unit.
4.  **Patient Chat**: Context-specific chat for a single patient's care team.
    *   You can send text notes.
    *   You can upload images (e.g., X-rays, wounds) by clicking the paperclip icon.

## Troubleshooting
*   **Camera not working**: Ensure you have granted browser permissions. Check that no other app is using the webcam.
*   **No Vitals detected**: Ensure the patient's face is well-lit and clearly visible.
*   **Real-time updates stopped**: Check your internet connection. A red "Disconnected" banner may appear.
